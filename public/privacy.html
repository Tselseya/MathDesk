Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

MathDesk is a free math-help website. This page explains what we collect, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how you can access or delete it. Questions or requests: chelseaandrea99@gmail.com.

What you can use without an account

You can ask the AI questions without logging in. Anonymous chats are not saved to our database. Your unsent draft and any lessons you save while logged out stay in your own browser (local storage) on your own device.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you sign up): your name, email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider).
  • Saved chats and lessons (only if logged in): the text of your conversations and any lessons you save, so you can see them on your devices.
  • What you send to the AI: the text you type, math you handwrite, calculator expressions you press Solve on, and any photo you choose to take or upload. Photos and drawings are used only to produce the answer. They are not saved to your account, and we do not keep image data in your chat history.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request information reach our hosting and AI workflow when you use the site. We use your IP address briefly to limit abuse (rate limiting). Those counters are kept for up to 24 hours.

We do not run advertising, do not sell your data, and do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Camera and uploads

The camera only starts after you allow it in your browser, and only on the capture screen. A photo is sent to the AI only when you press send. Please do not photograph people, IDs, or anything personal; crop to just the math problem.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase: sign-in and the database for logged-in users' chats and lessons.
  • Our AI workflow (n8n), reached through a secure tunnel, and Groq: the AI model provider that receives your question (and photo, if any) to write the answer. The workflow is set not to keep request contents in its history.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may keep standard server logs.
  • GeoGebra: if you open the graphing tool, GeoGebra loads inside the page and receives your browser information under its own policy.
  • Outbound links (for example Ko-fi or the learning resources in the Tools page) are governed by those sites' own policies.

Local storage on your device

MathDesk stores your sign-in session, unsent drafts, locally saved lessons and a service-worker cache (for faster loading) in your browser. Logging out removes your account's cached lessons and drafts from that browser.

How long we keep things

  • Saved chats: until you close that conversation tab (which deletes the saved copy) or delete your account.
  • Saved lessons: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Account: until you delete it.
  • Rate-limit counters: up to 24 hours.

Your choices

  • Download your data and delete your account from the “Signed in as …” menu while logged in. Deleting removes your account, saved chats and saved lessons.
  • Forgot your password? Use “Forgot your password?” on the log-in screen.
  • Email us at chelseaandrea99@gmail.com for any other request, including help if you cannot sign in.

Children

MathDesk is meant for learners, but accounts are for people old enough to agree to these terms in their country. If you are a parent or teacher and believe a child has given us personal information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, restrict each account to its own saved data, and limit what the AI service receives. No online service is perfectly secure, so please avoid putting personal or sensitive information into questions or photos.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
